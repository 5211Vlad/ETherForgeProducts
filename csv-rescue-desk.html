WHY IT EXISTS

Imports fail in boring places.

CSV Rescue Desk is for the gap between “I exported a CSV” and “I trust this file enough to import it somewhere important.”

01 · PREFLIGHT

Spot structural trouble early.

Detect common delimiters, duplicate headers, blank rows, exact duplicate rows, and uneven row widths before the next system touches the file.

02 · REPAIR

Apply deterministic cleanup.

Trim cells, normalize email case, repair duplicate headers deterministically, and write a cleaned copy rather than mutating the source.

03 · RECEIPT

Review what happened.

Use the QA output to see what the tool changed or flagged. The goal is inspectable cleanup, not a magic “trust me” button.

CURRENT INTERNAL EVIDENCE

RC2 hardening, not market proof.

Current EtherForge validation includes quoted multiline-field testing, uneven-row reporting, a 10k-row synthetic run, and source-preservation checks. These are internal engineering results, not external customer validation.

BOUNDARY

Structural rescue, not business inference.

CSV Rescue Desk does not invent missing values, infer business meaning, guarantee acceptance by every third-party importer, or replace a database migration plan.

GOOD, HONEST DATA WORK
