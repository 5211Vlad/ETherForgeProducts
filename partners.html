THE DEAL

Concrete beats promotional.

A useful collaboration starts with a bounded test: one part, one workflow, one device, one benchmark, or one prototype question. We document what worked, what failed, and what should happen next.

WHAT ETHERFORGE CAN PROVIDE

Evidence instead of theater.

Depending on the relationship: nonconfidential capability briefs, bounded evaluation plans, engineering feedback, build photos/video, benchmark summaries, public attribution, and documented prototype iteration.

We do not invent traction, savings, validation, AGI, sentience, or finished performance. Status stays visible: built, tested, pilot, evaluation, or external validation next.

FUNDING + COMMERCIALIZATION

Capital with a job to do.

We are interested in non-dilutive grants, paid pilots, startup credits, accelerators, competitions, seed/pre-seed investors, corporate innovation programs, laboratory access, and strategic partnerships relevant to practical AI infrastructure, developer tools, robotics and advanced manufacturing.

Initial conversations stay public-safe. Proprietary implementation, private GhostOS/MAGI architecture, unreleased CAD and patent-sensitive work remain outside cold outreach.
