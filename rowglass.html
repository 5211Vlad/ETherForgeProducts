QUESTIONS

Before you click.

Short answers to the questions that materially change whether this tool fits.

Does it upload my CSVs?

The product is positioned as a local-first comparison utility; the core workflow does not require an EtherForge cloud upload.

What is it for?

Pre-import checks, reconciliation, migration QA, and any workflow where you need to understand what changed between two tabular exports.

Is it live?

Yes. A live Gumroad product URL is recorded in the current EtherForge product ledger.
