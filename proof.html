THE CLAIM LADDER

Words have gates.

1
HypothesisAn idea worth testing. Not a product promise.
2
BuiltA runnable/customer artifact exists.
3
TestedDeclared tests have passed in the current environment.
4
LiveA public storefront/listing or customer route is verified.
5
Externally validatedIndependent/customer/provider evidence supports a stronger claim.
WHY THIS MATTERS

AI can repeat a claim faster than reality can validate it.

EtherForge’s research culture tries to prevent repetition from masquerading as corroboration. Public pages therefore separate product status, research status, and evidence status. The result is less exciting than hype—and much more useful for customers, partners, and investors deciding what is real today.
