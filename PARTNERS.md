# EtherForge Partnership / Resource Brief

EtherForge LLC is an Alabama-built software and applied AI R&D company. We ship narrow local-first utilities while developing longer-horizon AI continuity, robotics, perception, and fabrication systems.

Public site: https://etherforge-works.netlify.app/

## What we are looking for now

### Customer and pilot partners

- CSV / CRM migration teams willing to evaluate **RowGlass** or **CSV Rescue Desk**.
- Developer, agency, and operations teams willing to evaluate **Release Sentinel** or **Handoff Forge**.
- AI teams willing to run measured **METRON 2.0** context-efficiency / AI FinOps pilots.

### Hardware and fabrication partners

Useful support includes:

- PCs, laptops, mini-PCs, GPUs, local-inference hosts, and Linux workstations;
- FDM/SLA/SLS printers, 3D scanners, filament, resin, engineering materials, dryers, and post-processing equipment;
- machine-vision / depth cameras, sensors, IMUs, dev boards, SBCs, motor-control electronics, actuators, and robotics components;
- PCB/PCBA, CNC, laser, machining, metal-printing, and general fabrication credits;
- demo, refurbished, evaluation, loaner, test, or discontinued-but-functional equipment;
- lab, makerspace, metrology, prototyping, and independent test access.

We prefer a concrete evaluation over a vague sponsorship: define one part or system, document the setup, record what worked and failed, and publish an appropriate nonconfidential result.

### Funding and commercialization

We are interested in non-dilutive grants, startup credits, accelerators, competitions, paid pilots, seed/pre-seed investors, corporate innovation programs, and strategic partnerships that fit practical AI infrastructure, developer tools, robotics, advanced manufacturing, or local-first systems.

## What EtherForge can provide

Depending on the relationship:

- a concise nonconfidential capability brief;
- a bounded evaluation or demo plan;
- public build notes, photos, videos, or benchmark summaries where appropriate;
- engineering feedback from real prototype use;
- product attribution and partner acknowledgement;
- clearly separated public results without publishing proprietary architecture.

## Public-safety / IP boundary

We do not use initial outreach to disclose source code, private GhostOS/MAGI internals, unreleased CAD, credentials, patent-sensitive formulas, or unsupported AGI/sentience claims. Public claims stay tied to built, tested, pilot, evaluation, or validation status.
