# Mack's Modules — Intro Pricing Ladder

Status: CURRENT COMMERCIAL OPERATING RULE
Date: 2026-09-23

## Public introductory pricing
- Bench Check / Analysis: $19.99 one time.
- Quick Repair: from $59.
- Focused Repair: from $99.
- Mini-Build: from $149.
- Larger/uncertain work: scoped quote only.
- Paid Bench Check customers receive 20% off the labor portion of their first accepted repair/build quote.

## Discount boundary
The 20% first-build discount applies once to labor only. It does not reduce the Bench Check fee, taxes, hosting, software licenses, paid APIs, third-party services, hardware, shipping, or other pass-through costs. It is not automatically combined with other discounts.

## Price-rise rule
Do not increase pricing because of optimism alone. Review pricing after completed paid work creates real evidence.

Suggested review points:
- After 5 completed paid Bench Checks: consider Bench Check $24.99; Quick Repair from $69; Focused Repair from $119; Mini-Build from $179.
- After 15 completed paid Bench Checks or sustained backlog: consider Bench Check $29.99; Quick Repair from $79; Focused Repair from $139; Mini-Build from $199.
- After 30 completed paid Bench Checks, repeat customers, or clear demand: consider Bench Check $39.99; Quick Repair from $99; Focused Repair from $169; Mini-Build from $249.
- Mature target only when demand supports it: Bench Check $49+ and build pricing based on measured delivery time and support burden.

These are review triggers, not automatic changes.

## Evidence to track before any increase
- paid Bench Checks
- completion rate
- repair/build conversion rate
- average labor hours
- refund/dispute rate
- repeat-customer rate
- support burden
- customer objections
- customer-visible outcomes
- backlog / time-to-start

## Operating principle
Keep the first decision cheap. Earn the repair. Raise prices only when real customers demonstrate that Mack's is worth more.
